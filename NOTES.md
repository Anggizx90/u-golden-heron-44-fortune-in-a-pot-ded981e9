# Fortune in a Pot — notes

Competition: `u-golden-heron-44-fortune-in-a-pot-ded981e9` ("Fortune in a Pot").
Judge: z-ai/glm-5.3-flash, pairwise, criteria in order:
1. a fortune for a houseplant, about the plant's own future;
2. funny in a way a plant lover would laugh at — a small joke that lands, not a groaned-at pun;
3. kind and warm about plant and keeper — no doom, no withering, no mockery;
4. sounds like a fortune, prediction cadence, not a slogan/caption/care instruction;
5. one line ≤ 120 chars, reads cleanly aloud, no padding.
Plus the brief's kicker: a fortune that could be said to any plant in any room is
weaker than one written for this plant in this window.

## Winner

> Soon you will outgrow this pot so gracefully that your keeper will call it a promotion, not a repotting.

Why it wins the pairwise game:
- C1: unmistakably a houseplant's own future — this pot, this growth, no other subject fits.
- C2: the sharpest joke in the shortlist for a plant lover. Repotting is a real,
  familiar event; reframing it as a promotion is a small joke that lands, with no
  wordplay pun to groan at. It beats #7 ("pretend it was all their doing") and #11
  ("take notes") because the joke is the sentence's point, not its garnish.
- C3: growth framed as success and pride — no doom, no withering, keeper delighted.
- C4: "Soon you will…" is pure fortune cadence.
- C5: 104 characters, one line, reads cleanly aloud; nothing is padding.
- Specificity kicker: "this pot" ties it to the plant in front of the keeper, not any plant in any room.
